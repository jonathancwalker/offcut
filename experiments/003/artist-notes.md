# The Tidal Observatory

A red mullet sails over an observatory built from fungal lace and Brooklyn Bridge masonry. An airship’s skin becomes its moon, while shell specimens drift beyond the chart. Two small figures hold the scene at human scale.

## Material and composition

The collage uses 16 polygon fragments from five supplied images: Brooklyn Bridge, Fungi, Shells, Boarding an airship, and Red Mullet. All six source images were visually inspected before construction. The Frederick Douglass portrait was left unused.

The red fish provides the main color accent against a faceted circular fragment of the airship envelope. The lower arrangement combines a fungal canopy and stem, two bridge piers, a bridge deck, and a rectangle of historical natural-history text. Two shell specimens and two small observers extend the scene beyond the central forms.

## Observable revisions

- After the first canvas inspection, the printed foundation and fungal canopy were reduced, and the lower elements were raised to restore a complete paper margin.
- A separate bridge deck was placed behind the piers and fungal structure to connect the lower architecture.
- Two observers and two drifting shells were added to establish scale and balance the outer spaces.
- Final inspection led to small placement adjustments that closed gaps between the fish body and its four fins and joined the fungal stem to the canopy.

Five rendered stages were inspected during construction, including the completed composition. All artwork changes were made through the supplied client's `act()` function. Source cuts were accepted without overlap, and no material was reused.
