## Marsh marigold, 1896

Plate 7 from *Favourite Flowers of Garden and Greenhouse*, volume 1 (1896), by Edward Step; cultural directions by William Watson; plates selected and arranged by D. Bois. The digitized book came from Missouri Botanical Garden / Internet Archive. This illustrated-plant version is hosted by Wikimedia Commons and marked public domain (public-domain scan, author life plus 70 years, published before 1931 in the United States).

- [Source and rights record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marsh_Marigold-Favourite_Flowers_Garden_Greenhouse-1-0033-7.png)
