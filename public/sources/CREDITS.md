# Source material credits

These are existing historical source images, not AI-generated images. Copies are bundled locally so the worktable works without an external image service. The original three copies were resized to at most 1,500 pixels on their longest edge; the added collection below uses a maximum edge of 900 pixels. Copies are saved as JPEGs. No objects, colors, or compositions were generated or retouched.
